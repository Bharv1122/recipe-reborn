# Recipe Reborn: one label, one homemade recipe

Prepared October 21, 2026. Budget: $0. Publishing authorized by Beth for accessible accounts. No paid ads, new subscriptions, or account creation are part of this campaign.

## Audience and promise

Reach home cooks curious about making homemade versions of packaged favorites. Lead with one concrete action: bring an ingredient label and explore a recipe idea. Avoid health outcomes, guaranteed savings, exact taste matches, invented testimonials, and app-store availability claims.

## Offer and destination

Use the existing web experience at https://recipereborn.com/. The homepage and signup route returned HTTP 200 during this session's earlier checks. Do not promise unlimited access. Keep the first post focused on trying the product rather than pricing. Preview and signup are separate steps; saving may require an account.

## Distribution

1. Facebook: publish the question-led post on Beth's verified profile or Recipe Reborn account. Use the existing branded link-preview image.
2. Instagram: upload the existing branded image with the Instagram caption. A caption URL is not clickable; tell readers to type recipereborn.com. Do not claim a bio link exists without checking it.
3. X: publish the short post with its channel-specific link.
4. Reddit: inspect current rules and posting eligibility; use an explicitly allowed self-promotion or feedback thread. Disclose ownership. Do not scatter duplicate posts across cooking communities.
5. LinkedIn: use the founder-oriented post if Beth's account is accessible. Treat this as a secondary experiment, not evidence that founders are the strongest customer audience.

Verify the publishing identity before each submission. Check recent posts for this exact campaign to avoid duplicates. Record the permalink and read back the actual post after submission. An uncertain submission must be inspected before retrying.

## Success criteria and decisions

Targets, not forecasts: 10 independent participants, 5 participants saving a recipe, 3 returning within seven days. Track real payments separately from trials. The October 20 snapshot in Running Notes reported 152 visitors over 30 days and no accounts created in the preceding seven days; this is historical context, not a fresh measurement.

Use campaign links containing both src and standard UTM parameters. The local implementation stores rr_src as first touch, so existing attribution may remain attached to returning visitors. Anonymous funnel-event counts are not unique people and cannot prove account conversion. Review account and recipe records to substantiate activation; a second saved recipe is a retention proxy, not proof a person cooked it.

Do not enter zero for unavailable analytics. Use unknown until measured. Exclude owner/test accounts through a reviewed exclusion list before reporting acquisition. Never infer paid conversion from subscription tier alone.

If fewer than five independent visitors reach the product, work on distribution before judging the product. If at least ten participants arrive and fewer than three save a recipe, investigate first-use friction before increasing posting. If five save and none return after a full seven-day observation window, investigate repeat value. Small samples provide directional evidence only.
