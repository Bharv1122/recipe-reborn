# Recipe Reborn: first 10 users

Status: campaign prepared; no new post, message, ad, or invitation sent. Sale outreach is paused at Beth's direction. Updated September 12, 2026, Pacific.

## The decision

Recruit 10 new home cooks to make one recipe from a packaged food they already buy. Start with the existing web app and its free tier: three recipe generations per month, no card required, no app installation. Keep the offer and instructions simple. Do not advertise the newly coded mobile voice/photo changes as available on people's phones.

The first milestone is **10 new people making a first recipe**. Then aim for at least three of them to make another recipe within seven days of their first. These are goals, not results or forecasts. Ask separately whether they actually cooked a recipe; saved recipes do not prove cooking.

## Guide for an interested person

Use only in an approved reply or when the person has asked for help:

1. Open the link and create a free account. No community code or card is needed for the free tier.
2. Pick one packaged food you already have. In **Scan a Label**, choose **Take Photo** and photograph the printed ingredient list. Review what was read, then generate the recipe. If a photo isn't convenient, use **Type Ingredients** to paste the list.
3. Read the recipe, save it if useful, and tell us whether you'd cook it. If you get stuck, describe the screen and the last button you used.

Ask “May I check back once next week?” before a personal follow-up. A signup is not permission for a marketing email campaign. Replies and messages are drafts until explicitly authorized.

## Execution order

1. Confirm the social account, sign in there, and approve the exact invitation. Facebook is currently signed out in the available in-app browser. Verify the resulting post and link after publication.
2. Read genuine replies and help willing volunteers finish one recipe. Keep feedback in their chosen channel. Do not scrape addresses, mass-message contacts or repeat the old promotion automatically.
3. Review results after 48 hours of the post being live. If no one responds, change the invitation or try a relevant cooking community that explicitly permits founder recruitment; get its rules and permission first. Do not buy traffic to compensate for an unproven first-use flow.
4. After seven days, count first recipes, self-reported cooking and second-recipe activity. Fix the most common point of confusion before expanding the audience.

No recurring monitor is configured. The 48-hour and seven-day checks are campaign steps, not scheduled promises.
