# Recipe Reborn — Vibe Coding Academy Hackathon submission kit

## Judge-facing story

**One-line pitch:** Recipe Reborn turns a processed ingredient label into a generated fresh recipe, a transparent before-and-after view, and practical next steps for saving, planning, and shopping.

**Problem:** Ingredient labels are difficult to translate into an achievable homemade alternative. People can find warnings or generic recipes, but the work of understanding the label, choosing substitutions, structuring a recipe, and organizing the next shopping trip is still fragmented.

**Target user:** Busy home cooks who want to understand a packaged product and explore a fresh version that fits their preferences and available time.

**Unique transformation:** A user can photograph, scan, paste, or describe a packaged-food label. Recipe Reborn identifies relevant label items, generates a structured recipe with a fresh ingredient list, and shows the two sides separately so the user can see what changed. After reviewing the result, a signed-in user can save it, organize it in a collection, add it to a meal plan, and build a shopping list.

**Why now:** Multimodal AI can read labels and return structured cooking output quickly enough for a focused consumer workflow. Recipe Reborn combines that capability with validation, safe cancellation, transparent progress feedback, and explicit user-controlled saving.

**Call to action:** Bring one ingredient label and try the public example at https://recipereborn.com/hackathon. Create an account only if you want to generate and organize your own recipes.

Recipe outputs are cooking starting points, not medical advice. Cost information, when shown, is identified as an AI estimate and actual prices vary.

## Acquisition and channel plan

1. Publish short label-to-recipe demonstrations on TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts, each centered on one recognizable packaged item.
2. Use QR cards and live demos with independent grocers, cooking classes, community kitchens, and other food-education settings.
3. Build search pages and articles around specific packaged-food alternatives and dietary preferences without making unsupported health claims.
4. Add referral moments around saved recipes and shareable collections once retention and privacy behavior are measured.
5. Track the funnel from public example to signup, first generation, first save, and first planning action. Optimize for completed transformations, not clicks alone.
