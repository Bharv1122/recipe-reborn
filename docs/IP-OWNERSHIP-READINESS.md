# Recipe Reborn IP ownership readiness inventory

Prepared August 24, 2026 from the project repository and connected development-account metadata. This is an evidence inventory for counsel, not a legal opinion or a conclusion that ownership is verified.

## Executive finding

No repository evidence shows an obvious outside human contributor with current GitHub access or a direct ownership claim. However, the chain of title is not documentation-ready. The largest gaps are the restored Abacus.AI archive, AI-assisted authorship, original logo/source files, commercial-video inputs, and the absence of assignment/license records. A qualified California business/IP attorney should resolve those gaps before Recipe Reborn represents that it exclusively owns all prize-submission IP.

The repository is public while its README describes a private project. There is no root project license. Public visibility does not itself document ownership or grant an express project license, and the mismatch should be reviewed intentionally.

### Domain and production control signals

- `recipereborn.com` is attached to the connected Vercel project `recipe-reborn`; Vercel records the project under RecipeReborn's projects and a Beth-linked creator identity.
- Public RDAP lists Cloudflare as registrar, registration on October 24, 2025, and expiration on October 24, 2026. Registrant identity is not public, so registrar ownership and renewal control are not proven by this audit.
- Preserve the Cloudflare registrar receipt, account ownership, renewal confirmation, DNS history, and Vercel project ownership/export.

### Branding and product use

- The name `Recipe Reborn` and `recipereborn.com` are used throughout the product, legal pages, metadata, store drafts, and repository history.
- The Terms page asserts that the service is protected by copyright and trademark law, but the repo contains no trademark clearance report, application, registration, assignment, or common-law-use evidence packet.
- `public/logo.png` and `public/logo-text-hero.png` enter history under the generic earliest author identity. `public/logo-mark.png` is documented as a later crop/derivative of that logo. The original design file, creator statement, prompts, license, or paid receipt is not in the repo.

### Media and assets

- The repository contains three production videos and posters. Git records when they were added, but not the source project files, tool/account terms, voice/music licenses, model/property releases, or an input/output manifest.
- The Cooper and Riker commercial should have a record identifying the source dog photos/video, who captured them, any AI generation/editing services, voice/music sources, and permission from every human whose voice, likeness, property, or performance appears.
- Mobile brand icons derive from project logo files. The mobile tree also retains Expo/React template graphics. Confirm which template assets ship, preserve their notices, and remove unused template branding before store screenshots/final binary if appropriate.

### Software licenses

- Root `package-lock.json` has 115 direct runtime/development dependencies: 98 marked MIT, 9 Apache-2.0, 3 ISC, 2 BSD-2-Clause, 1 Unlicense, Mapbox GL with its included license file, and one package without a lockfile license field (`@next/swc-wasm-nodejs`). No direct dependency was labeled GPL, AGPL, LGPL, MPL, EPL, or CDDL in this mechanical check.
- Mobile has 34 direct runtime/development dependencies: 33 MIT and 1 Apache-2.0.
- This direct-dependency count is not a full transitive-license opinion. Produce an SBOM/notices bundle for the exact release artifact and have counsel review notice, attribution, source-offer, font, media, API-data, and commercial-use obligations.
- `mobile/LICENSE` is the Expo template MIT notice naming 650 Industries. It documents Expo template licensing; it should not be presented as Recipe Reborn's project-level ownership statement. Counsel should decide whether to retain it as a third-party notice and what project-level notice/license is appropriate.

1. Who created the first working product and every major pre-Git/archive component, in what capacity, and under what account/terms?
2. Did any employee, contractor, friend, agency, contest teammate, or household member contribute code, recipes, copy, logo work, photography, audio, or video?
3. Were those contributions assigned in writing, created within employment, or licensed with sufficient commercial/prize rights?
4. What Abacus.AI, Anthropic/Claude, OpenAI/Codex, image/video/voice generator, stock-media, font, and design-tool terms applied on each creation date?
5. Which portions reflect documented human authorship versus substantially machine-generated output?
6. Who controls the GitHub, Cloudflare/domain, Vercel, Expo, Apple, Google Play, Stripe, Supabase, email, analytics, and AI-provider accounts, and should any rights be assigned to a business later?
7. Has `Recipe Reborn`, confusingly similar wording, the logo, and related food/recipe software and services received a comprehensive federal, California/state, domain, app-store, and common-law clearance search?
8. Does any recipe text, nutrition/product data, barcode data, packaging image, or generated output reproduce protected third-party expression or violate an API/data license?

## Attorney-ready questions and documents

Give counsel:

- This inventory, full Git history/bundle, repository settings/export, and a commit-author alias map.
- The original Abacus archive plus hashes, handoff/export emails, receipts, and creation-date terms.
- Every contributor/contractor/employment document and a candid list of unwritten contributions.
- Logo/design source files, prompt/revision history, media source projects, source photos, audio stems, and all tool/subscription receipts and terms.
- Domain, hosting, store, database, billing, and AI-service account ownership records.
- Exact dependency SBOM, license texts/notices, fonts, APIs/datasets, and commercial-use terms.
- Dated first-use evidence and a description of the actual goods/services offered under the mark.
- The Purina prize rules, submission content, team-member list, and every ownership/indemnity/license warranty the entrant must make.

Ask counsel:

1. Who presently owns each category of code, brand, media, domain, data rights, and contest submission, and what evidence supports that chain?
2. Which assignments, confirmatory assignments, licenses, releases, or work-made-for-hire acknowledgments are needed, and from whom?
3. Should rights remain with the individual or be assigned to a future entity, and in what order relative to prize entry, Apple/Google enrollment, trademark filing, or investment?
4. What should be done about the public repository/no-root-license mismatch and the Expo template license file?
5. Which AI-assisted portions are protectable human-authored expression, what disclosures would a copyright application require, and what creation records should be preserved?
6. Is the Recipe Reborn word mark/logo clear for the actual classes and channels, and who is the correct owner/applicant if a filing is recommended?
7. Should source code, website text, logo art, or audiovisual works be registered separately, and which versions/deposits should be used?
8. Do third-party packages, APIs, datasets, packaging images, recipes, music, voices, photos, or generated media require additional notices, permissions, or removal?
9. Can the intended prize applicant truthfully make every ownership, authority, originality, and non-infringement warranty in the current rules?

## Preserve now; do not file yet

Create a read-only evidence folder outside the public repo containing dated exports, original files, hashes, receipts, terms, and account screenshots. Do not rewrite Git history, discard originals, backdate assignments, or submit trademark/copyright forms until counsel identifies the correct owner, authors, disclosures, and specimens.
