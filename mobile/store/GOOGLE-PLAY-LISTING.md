## Full description

Reimagine your favorite processed foods as healthier homemade recipes—with more control over what goes into every bite.

Recipe Reborn helps you make homemade alternatives to the packaged foods you love. Scan a product barcode, photograph its food label, or describe a favorite food to get a recipe you can cook yourself. Review the ingredients, follow the cooking steps, and compare the original product's nutrition with estimates for your homemade version when data is available.

MAKE YOUR FAVORITES AT HOME

Explore homemade versions of familiar packaged meals, snacks, sauces, and more. Adapt recipes around your food preferences, ingredients you want to avoid, and the way you like to cook.

SEE WHAT GOES INTO YOUR FOOD

Keep the ingredient list, cooking instructions, and available nutrition comparisons together when you save a recipe, so you can return to the same details later.

BRING IT INTO YOUR EVERYDAY ROUTINE

Save recipes in collections, plan meals, and build shopping lists. Ask Chef for cooking help. You can also speak ingredients or photograph your fridge or pantry for ideas using food you already have. Review and correct photo results before saving them. Shopping-list check-offs work offline and sync when you reconnect.

AI suggestions and product data can be incomplete or incorrect. Nutrition values for homemade recipes are estimates; results depend on ingredients and portions. Always check ingredients, allergens, preparation, and food safety before cooking.
