## Short description

Create fresh recipes from ingredient lists and your reviewed pantry inventory.

## Full description

Recipe Reborn turns the ingredients around you into practical cooking ideas.

Generate and save recipes, scan product barcodes, organize collections and meal plans, and keep a shopping list available even when your connection drops. Photograph a refrigerator or pantry to create a draft inventory; you must review and correct it before anything is saved.

Your account's allergy preferences, disliked ingredients, access level, trial, and safety checks remain controlled by Recipe Reborn's server.

AI and barcode sources can be incomplete or incorrect. Always verify ingredients, allergens, preparation, and food safety before cooking.
