## Promotional text

## Description

Recipe Reborn helps you cook with the ingredients already around you.

- Generate fresh-food recipes from a packaged ingredient list or reviewed pantry inventory.
- Scan barcodes and look up available product ingredient information.
- Photograph your refrigerator or pantry, correct the draft inventory, then save only the list you confirm.
- Save recipes and organize them into collections and meal plans.
- Create shopping lists and check items off even when you are offline.
- Keep your account's allergy, dislike, Premium, trial, and safety rules enforced by Recipe Reborn's server.

AI can make mistakes. Always review ingredients, allergens, preparation, and food safety before cooking.
